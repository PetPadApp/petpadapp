Who We Are

Vici Commons, LLC is a software company based in Las Cruces, New Mexico, dedicated to bringing people together through thoughtful digital tools. Our goal is simple: to help communities share the important details about the loved ones in their care — so that everyone involved can provide the best possible support.

Our name is inspired by the village commons — the shared gathering place at the heart of every community — and by the well-known adage that it takes a village. We believe caring for a loved one is never a solo effort. When the people around them have accurate, up-to-date information at their fingertips, they can step in with confidence and provide the highest level of care, tailored to each individual.

Our First Product: Pet Pad

Pets are family. And just like any loved one, they rely on a village — pet sitters, friends, family members, vets, and rescues — to keep them happy, healthy, and safe. When someone else steps in to care for your pet, they deserve to know the details that matter: feeding routines, medications, quirks, comforts, and the little things that help your pet feel secure.

That's why we built Pet Pad: a secure, all-in-one companion for organizing your pet's complete profile and sharing exactly what caregivers need. With PIN-protected share links, your pet's village always has the right information at the right time — so you can relax knowing they're in good hands.
